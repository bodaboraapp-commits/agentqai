The five samples

Ordered from most technical to most editorial.

Click “Open full page” for the real thing
01 — CONSOLE

Console Dark · product-led

A live-call console is the hero. The page sells the product by showing it running: transcript, latency, outcome and credits.

Best for technical buyers, ops teams and developers

  • Feels like a tool you can log into today, not a brochure
  • Bento feature grid and a blunt comparison table
  • Strong “show, don’t tell” for demos and AI crawlers
Open full page → Preview
02 — LEDGER

Ledger Editorial · answer-first

A product overview written like a publication. The definition, key facts, comparison, glossary and FAQ are the design.

Best for SEO, AI Overviews and considered buyers

  • Answer-first “quick answer” + at-a-glance key facts
  • Glossary with DefinedTerm schema — highly extractable
  • “Cite this page” line and a visible last-updated date
Open full page → Preview
03 — ORBIT

Orbit Gradient · conversion

A modern AI-SaaS aesthetic with a dashboard preview, use-case tabs and a credits calculator that turns pricing into a decision.

Best for paid traffic, demos and fast conversion

  • Interactive credits calculator lowers pricing anxiety
  • Inbound / outbound / SMS tabs show three products in one
  • Most familiar “AI startup” pattern — lowest explanation cost
Open full page → Preview
04 — HANDOFF

Handoff Warm · human

The product story is a conversation: the AI answers, the caller asks for a person, the call transfers warm. Built on trust, not tech.

Best for SMBs, clinics, services and non-technical buyers

  • Handoff is the headline feature, not a footnote
  • Warm, approachable tone that reduces AI distrust
  • Friendly microcopy: “Credits, not contracts”
Open full page → Preview
05 — NORTH

North Minimal · enterprise

Hairline rules, oversized type and numbered sections. The calm, procurement-friendly direction — closer to Linear or Stripe than to a startup launch page.

Best for enterprise, regulated buyers and brand credibility

  • Reads as an established company, not an experiment
  • Pricing as a clean table — easy to compare and to quote
  • Fastest to load and the easiest to keep accessible

My recommendation

If the goal is to be found and quoted — by Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity — 02 Ledger is the strongest single choice: its whole structure is extractable. If the goal is demo requests and paid traffic, 03 Orbit converts fastest. For enterprise procurement, 05 North carries the most credibility. The best hybrid for AgentQ AI: Ledger’s content spine, Console’s live-call product moment, and Orbit’s calculator.
