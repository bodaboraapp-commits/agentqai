01 — Definition

A calling platform, not a dialer with AI bolted on.

AgentQ AI was built around the conversation itself: the audio, the reasoning, the handoff and the follow-up.

AgentQ AI is an AI voice agent platform. It answers inbound calls, places outbound calls, sends and receives SMS, and runs follow-up sequences — using the same contact list, the same analytics and the same prepaid credit balance. An agent listens through speech-to-text, decides what to say with a language model grounded in your knowledge base, and replies with natural text-to-speech. When the conversation needs a person, it transfers with the transcript attached.

The commercial model is deliberately simple. There is no per-seat licence and no annual contract: you buy credits, and one credit covers one connected call. Failed, busy or unanswered calls cost nothing, and unused credits never expire.

“The question is not whether AI can answer a phone call. It is whether it can finish the job — and know when to stop.”AgentQ AI product principle

What AgentQ AI is not

  • Not an IVR. Callers are not pushed through a keypad tree; they talk, interrupt and change their mind.
  • Not a chatbot on a phone line. The agent has a goal, a knowledge base and rules for escalation.
  • Not a seat licence. You are not buying per-agent software seats. You buy connected calls.

03 — Anatomy of a call

What happens in the half-second before the agent speaks.

The loop below runs continuously for the length of the call. It is the difference between a recording and a conversation.

  1. 01 / 05

    Answer

    The platform picks up, plays the greeting and starts streaming audio both ways.

    Voice channel
  2. 02 / 05

    Listen

    Speech-to-text transcribes the caller in real time, including interruptions and accents.

    Speech-to-text
  3. 03 / 05

    Decide

    A language model combines the transcript, your script and the knowledge base to choose the next move.

    LLM + knowledge base
  4. 04 / 05

    Speak

    Text-to-speech answers in the voice you selected. Typical responses land in under a second.

    Text-to-speech
  5. 05 / 05

    Act

    Book, qualify, send an SMS, update the CRM or transfer to a person — with the transcript attached.

    Outcome

07 — Glossary

The five terms this page uses.

So nobody has to guess what a credit, a connected call or a handoff actually means.

AI voice agent
Software that holds a spoken conversation in real time using speech-to-text, a language model and text-to-speech.
Connected call
A call that reaches a person or voicemail and is handled by the platform. Only connected calls consume a credit.
Credit
The prepaid unit used by AgentQ AI. One credit covers one connected call, and credits never expire.
Human handoff
A rules-based transfer from an AI agent to a person, including the live transcript and call context.
Knowledge base
Uploaded documents and answers that ground the agent in your facts instead of generic guesses.
08 — Questions

Answers to the questions buyers actually ask.

These are the same answers the agents give on a call — short, specific and grounded.

What is AgentQ AI?
AgentQ AI is a cloud platform for AI voice agents, automated calling campaigns and two-way SMS. Its agents hold real conversations in 40+ languages, qualify leads, book appointments and transfer to people. Billing is pay-as-you-go on prepaid credits with no subscription, and credits never expire.
What is an AI voice agent?
An AI voice agent is software that holds a spoken phone conversation without a human on the line. It combines speech-to-text, a language model and text-to-speech to listen, decide and answer in real time, then acts — booking, qualifying or transferring the call.
How much does an AI call cost?
One credit covers one connected call. Credits cost $0.10 each on the 100-credit pack, $0.09 on the 500-credit pack and $0.08 on the 1,000-credit pack. Failed, busy or unanswered calls are free.
Do credits expire?
No. AgentQ AI credits never expire. Buy them once and use them for campaigns, inbound agents, SMS or sequences whenever you need them.
Which languages can the agents speak?
Voice agents support 40+ languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Swahili, Chinese and Japanese. The account interface is available in 10 languages.
Can an AI agent transfer a call to a human?
Yes. Transfers happen on rules you set — a request for a human, a specific intent, a sentiment threshold or a high-value lead. The person who receives the call gets the live transcript and context.
Do I need a Twilio or Plivo account?
No. AgentQ AI supports Twilio, Plivo and Africa’s Talking out of the box, including number purchase. Existing carrier contracts can be kept by bringing your own numbers or connecting a SIP trunk.
Can the agent use our own documents?
Yes. Upload documents to the knowledge base and agents answer from your content. When an answer is not in the knowledge base, the agent says so instead of guessing, and can transfer to a person.
Is AgentQ AI compliant and secure?
The platform is designed to support GDPR and TCPA-aligned calling: do-not-call handling, consent records, role-based access, two-factor authentication, KYC and encryption in transit and at rest. Compliance teams should confirm the configuration for their market.
How long does setup take?
Most teams connect a number and make their first AI call in under an hour. Build the agent from a template, upload your knowledge base, choose a voice, then launch a campaign or point an inbound number at the agent.
