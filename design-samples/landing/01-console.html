Questions, answered

What buyers ask before the first campaign.

Short, direct answers — the same ones our agents give on a call.

What is AgentQ AI?
AgentQ AI is a cloud platform for AI voice agents, automated calling campaigns and two-way SMS. Its agents hold real conversations in 40+ languages, qualify leads, book appointments and transfer to people. Billing is pay-as-you-go on prepaid credits with no subscription, and credits never expire.
How do AI voice agents actually work?
Each call runs a real-time loop: speech-to-text transcribes the caller, a language model decides the reply using your script and knowledge base, and text-to-speech answers in a natural voice. Responses typically arrive in under a second, and every call is transcribed and scored.
Do I need my own Twilio or Plivo account?
No. AgentQ AI supports Twilio, Plivo and Africa’s Talking out of the box, including number purchase. Teams with existing carrier contracts can bring their own numbers or connect an existing SIP trunk.
Which languages are supported?
Voice agents support 40+ languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Swahili, Chinese and Japanese. The account interface is available in 10 languages.
How much does a call cost?
One credit covers one connected call. Credits start at $10 for 100 calls ($0.10 per call) and fall to $0.08 per call on the 1,000-credit pack. Failed, busy or unanswered calls are free. Volume pricing starts at 5,000 credits.
Do credits expire?
No. Credits never expire. Buy them once and use them for campaigns, inbound agents, SMS or sequences whenever you need them.
Can the AI transfer a call to a human?
Yes. Agents transfer on rules you set — a request for a human, a specific intent, a sentiment threshold or a high-value lead. The person who picks up gets the live transcript and call context.
Is AgentQ AI compliant and secure?
The platform is designed to support GDPR and TCPA-aligned calling: do-not-call handling, consent records, role-based access, two-factor authentication, KYC and encryption in transit and at rest. Your compliance team should confirm the configuration for your market.
How long does setup take?
Most teams connect a number and make their first AI call in under an hour. Build the agent from a template, upload your knowledge base, choose a voice, then launch a campaign or point an inbound number at the agent.
Can I use AgentQ AI with my CRM?
Yes. CRM webhooks and a REST API with OpenAPI documentation push call outcomes, transcripts, bookings and sentiment into your CRM, help desk or warehouse.
