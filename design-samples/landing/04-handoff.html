Questions

The things people ask before their first call.

What is AgentQ AI?
A cloud platform for AI voice agents, automated calling campaigns and two-way SMS. Agents hold real conversations in 40+ languages, qualify, book and transfer — billed pay-as-you-go with credits that never expire.
Does the AI sound like a robot?
Agents use modern text-to-speech voices and a language model rather than a fixed script. They handle interruptions, ask one question at a time, match the caller’s language and say they are an AI when asked.
Can a caller speak to a human?
Yes. Callers can ask for a person at any point, and you can set rules for automatic handoff based on intent, sentiment or lead value. The human receives the live transcript and context.
How much does a call cost?
One credit per connected call: $0.10 on the 100 pack, $0.09 on the 500 pack and $0.08 on the 1,000 pack. Failed, busy or unanswered calls are free.
Do credits expire?
No. Use them for campaigns, inbound agents, SMS or sequences whenever you need them.
Which languages are supported?
40+ voice languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Swahili, Chinese and Japanese. The interface is available in 10 languages.
Do I need my own Twilio or Plivo account?
No. Twilio, Plivo and Africa’s Talking are supported, including number purchase. You can also bring numbers or connect a SIP trunk.
How long does setup take?
Most teams make their first AI call in under an hour: connect a number, build the agent, launch a campaign or point an inbound line at it.
