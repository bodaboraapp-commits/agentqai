06 — Questions

What buyers ask first.

Short answers, grounded in what the product actually does.

What is AgentQ AI?+
A cloud platform for AI voice agents, automated calling campaigns and two-way SMS. Its agents hold real conversations in 40+ languages, qualify leads, book appointments and transfer to people — billed pay-as-you-go on prepaid credits with no subscription, and credits never expire.
How do AI voice agents work?+
Speech-to-text transcribes the caller, a language model decides the reply from your script and knowledge base, and text-to-speech answers. Responses typically arrive in under a second, and every call is transcribed and scored.
How much does a call cost?+
One credit per connected call: $0.10 on the 100 pack, $0.09 on the 500 pack and $0.08 on the 1,000 pack. Failed, busy or unanswered calls are free.
Do credits expire?+
No. Buy once and use them for campaigns, inbound agents, SMS or sequences whenever you need them.
Which languages are supported?+
40+ voice languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Swahili, Chinese and Japanese. The interface is available in 10 languages.
Can the AI transfer to a human?+
Yes — on rules you set: a request for a person, a specific intent, a sentiment threshold or a high-value lead. The human receives the transcript and call context.
Do I need my own Twilio or Plivo account?+
No. Twilio, Plivo and Africa’s Talking are supported, including number purchase. You can also bring numbers or connect a SIP trunk.
Is AgentQ AI secure and compliant?+
The platform is designed to support GDPR and TCPA-aligned calling: do-not-call handling, consent records, role-based access, two-factor authentication, KYC and encryption in transit and at rest.
